PonDashi Player Video Dual

A Mac app for instant playout of video, stills and audio on two channels. Run Player 1 and Player 2 side by side, and preview what’s next before you take it to air.

In developmentDetails may change Mac (Apple silicon)

Key features

From left: playlist, NEXT and on-air monitor — two identical players, one above the other. (Interface in Japanese.)

ChooseBefore and during the show

  • Playlists

    Three per player. Drag to add and reorder; “Black” sends black.

  • NEXT preview

    Check the next clip without affecting what’s on air.

PlayOn air

  • Three modes

    Manual (select, then take), Direct (click to play) and Loop-through (advances automatically).

  • Fades

    IN and OUT from 0 to 5 seconds; video and audio fade together.

RouteOutputs

  • Outputs

    External displays or SDI output devices, with a separate audio device per player.

  • Output mask

    Crop the picture to suit each venue, such as portrait screens (Player 2).

Supported files

Videomp4, mov, mxf, mts, m2ts and more
Stillsjpg, png, tiff and more (with a set on-air duration)
Audio onlywav, aiff, mp3, m4a and more (audio over black)
