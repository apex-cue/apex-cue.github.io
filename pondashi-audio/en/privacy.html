Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026 · Revised: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how PonDashi Player Audio (“the app”), a Mac app provided by KENJI morimoto (“the developer”), handles your information. If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information or other data. However, the version purchased on our website (licensed version) sends only the information described in “7. Version purchased on our website”.

  • There is no account registration.
  • There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party (except license registration and deregistration in the licensed version).

2. Network access

The App Store version does not connect to the internet. The licensed version connects only when registering or deregistering a license key.

3. Audio files and show files

Audio files you load and show files you save are handled only on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere.

4. Information stored on your Mac

To work, the app stores the following on your Mac. All of it stays on your Mac and is never sent anywhere.

  • Settings (window position, output devices, last opened show)
  • EQ presets
  • Converted copies of loaded audio, for faster loading next time (can be deleted from the menu; only in versions distributed outside the App Store)
  • Permission data used to reopen your files (App Store version)

You can remove this by deleting the app and its related data.

5. Microphone

The app never records. Only when an output device’s name can’t be read any other way, it may ask once for macOS microphone permission in order to show the device name. Normally it does not ask. Even if you allow it, no audio is recorded, stored or sent.

6. Crash reports

The app does not send crash reports of its own. Sharing of diagnostic data by macOS follows your Mac’s settings (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements).

7. Version purchased on our website (licensed version)

Only when a license key is registered or deregistered, the app sends the license key, a number that identifies the Mac (a hash derived from the Mac’s unique identifier; the original identifier is not sent) and the Mac’s computer name to our license server (operated on an external cloud service). This information is used only to manage the number of licensed Macs. Audio files and show contents are never sent.

Payments are processed by a payment service provider; we never receive your card details. The email address used at purchase is used to reissue licenses and to respond to inquiries.

The App Store version continues to send no data.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
