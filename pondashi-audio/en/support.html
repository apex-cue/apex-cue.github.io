Frequently asked questions

A cue won’t play, or says it needs permission

The App Store version runs in the macOS sandbox, so it can’t read files in folders it hasn’t been given access to. Use the bar at the top of the window to grant access to the folder that holds your audio (「素材のフォルダを許可…」 — “Allow audio folder…”). The files are reloaded once access is granted.

Files added by drag and drop may need this the next time you launch the app.

Which audio formats can it read?

WAV, AIFF, CAF, MP3, M4A (AAC), FLAC, Ogg and more. The App Store version cannot read Apple Lossless (ALAC); export those files to WAV or AIFF first.

How do I choose output devices?

Open Output Settings (「出力設定…」) and choose a device for main and for HP (monitor). With on-air turned on, audio goes to main; with it off, main is muted and audio goes to HP.

I can’t add or edit cues

The app opens in play-only mode (CONFIG off) to prevent mistakes during a show. Press the CONFIG button at the top to turn it on; then you can add, delete and reorder cues, edit waveforms, use the EQ and more.

Why is it asking for microphone access?

It never records. Only when an output device’s name can’t be read any other way, the app may ask once for microphone access so it can show the device name. Playback works without it; device names may just not appear.
