Support

How to connect, common questions and contact details for PonDashi Remote (iPhone and iPad).

Contact

Please email bug reports and questions to:

info.apexcue@gmail.com

Including your iPhone/iPad model and iOS version, the version of PonDashi Player Audio PRO on the Mac, and what happened helps us find the cause faster. This app is developed and run by one person, so a reply may take a few days.

What you need

  • iPhone or iPad (iOS 17 or later)
  • “PonDashi Player Audio PRO” for Mac (sold separately)
  • The Mac and the iPhone/iPad on the same network (wireless or wired LAN)

How to connect

  1. In PonDashi Player Audio PRO on the Mac, open “Remote…” from the menu (or the screen) and turn it ON.
  2. If the Mac asks whether to allow incoming connections, click Allow.
  3. Open PonDashi Remote on the iPhone or iPad. The first time, if it asks to find and connect to devices on your local network, tap Allow.
  4. Choose your Mac under “PonDashi on this network”.
  5. Enter the 4-digit PIN shown in “Remote…” on the Mac. You won’t need it again next time.

Troubleshooting

The Mac doesn’t appear in the list

Check that “Remote…” is ON on the Mac and that the Mac and the iPhone/iPad are on the same network.

On the iPhone/iPad, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure PonDashi Remote is ON.

If it still doesn’t appear, enter the address shown in “Remote…” on the Mac (for example 192.168.1.20:8770) in “Connect by address” in the app.

It asks for a PIN

Choosing “New PIN” on the Mac disconnects devices that were connected. Enter the new PIN shown in “Remote…” on the Mac.

I entered the wrong PIN several times

Wait 30 seconds and try again.

The cue fader doesn’t move

Tap the gear at the top right → turn “Use cue fader” ON. It starts OFF so a stray touch won’t change the volume.

The connection dropped

The app reconnects automatically. A green dot at the top means connected; orange means reconnecting.

I want to try it without a Mac

Use “Demo” at the bottom of the first screen.

I want to use a wired connection

Connect the iPad to the same network as the Mac with a USB LAN adapter.
