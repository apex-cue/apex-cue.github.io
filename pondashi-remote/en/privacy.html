Privacy policy

Effective: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how “PonDashi Remote” (the “App”), an iPhone and iPad app provided by KENJI morimoto (the “Developer”), handles your information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information or any other data.

  • There is no account registration.
  • No data is sent to servers of the Developer or any third party.
  • The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

2. Network communication

The App communicates only with the Mac you choose on the same network (PonDashi Player Audio PRO). It does not communicate with servers on the internet.

  • Sent to the Mac: your operations (pads, GO, stop, cue volume, etc.) and the PIN
  • Received from the Mac: playback status (cue names, time remaining, etc.)

3. Local network permission

The App uses the local network permission only to find and control the Mac on the same network.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on your iPhone or iPad so that it can work. All of it stays on the device and is never sent elsewhere.

  • The PIN (stored in the device’s keychain)
  • The name and address of the Mac you last connected to
  • App settings (whether the cue fader is used)

These are removed when you delete the App.

5. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new content and date will be posted on this page.
