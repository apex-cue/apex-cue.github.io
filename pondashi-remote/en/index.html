PonDashi Remote

A remote control for “PonDashi Player Audio PRO” on the Mac, from your iPhone or iPad. Fire and stop pads, GO on the playlist, stop all, and set the cue volume — right in your hand.

App StoreComing soon FreeNo in-app purchases iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later)

What you can do

Keep running the show even away from the Mac.

FirePads

  • Pads

    Fire and stop 3 pages × 12 pads.

  • Stop all

    Stop everything that’s playing with one tap.

SendPlaylist

  • Select and GO

    Select a cue in the playlist, GO and stop.

AdjustVolume

  • Cue fader

    Sets the volume of the cue that’s playing (or, if nothing is playing, the selected cue or pad). It only moves when “Use cue fader” is ON in the app’s settings, so a stray touch won’t change the level. Changes are saved to the show on the Mac.

SeeStatus

  • At a glance

    Playing or fading out, time remaining and progress, shortcuts, LOOP and MANUAL play modes.

  • Demo mode

    No Mac? Try the screens with “Demo” at the bottom of the first screen.

Requirements

Please check the following before use. How to connect is on the support page.

DevicesiPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later)
Also required“PonDashi Player Audio PRO” for Mac (sold separately). The app can’t control anything without PRO.
NetworkThe Mac and the iPhone/iPad must be on the same network (wireless or wired LAN). iPad can also connect by wire with a USB LAN adapter.
