Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026 (provisional; to be finalized at release)

This policy explains how ApexCue Transfer (“the app”), provided by KENJI morimoto (“the developer”), handles your information. If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information or other data. There is no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no data is sent to the developer or any third party.

2. Network access

The app communicates only with the devices you specify (Q-SYS® Core, NAS, FTP server) to list, load and transfer audio files. It connects to nothing else.

3. Audio files

Audio files are handled only between your devices and the destinations you choose. They are never sent to the developer.

4. Information stored on your computer

The app stores the following on your computer. None of it is sent to the developer or any third party.

  • Connection details (address, user name and password for the Q-SYS® Core, NAS and FTP server)
  • Settings
  • Transfer records (transferred/saved marks and timestamps)

Connection details are used only to sign in to those destinations. If the computer is shared, use separate OS user accounts.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
