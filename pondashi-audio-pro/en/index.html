PonDashi Player Audio PRO

PonDashi Player Audio PRO was developed to meet the needs of every kind of show. Testers already working in professional productions shaped what went into it.

The advanced edition of PonDashi Player Audio. LTC (timecode) input and output are available as an option (sold separately).

Coming soon Mac

What PRO adds

Everything in PonDashi Player Audio is included. LTC input and output are an option (sold separately). (Interface in Japanese.)

SoundProcessing

  • Native Core Audio

    Native support for Core Audio on the Mac, with carefully designed internal processing for high-quality sound.

RouteOUT buses

  • Output bus routing

    Assign a destination for each output (OUT) bus.

ReceiveLTC input (option)

  • Chase LTC

    Receive LTC on an audio interface input and fire each cue exactly at the timecode set in its timer field.

  • Frame rates

    Switch between 29.97 DF, 29.97 NDF, 30 and more.

SendLTC output (option)

  • Generate LTC

    Send timecode from a chosen output channel to sync lighting, video and other equipment.

ControlRemote

  • Control it from iPhone and iPad

    With the free remote app “PonDashi Remote”, fire pads, GO on the playlist, stop all and set the cue volume from your hand (coming soon to the App Store).
